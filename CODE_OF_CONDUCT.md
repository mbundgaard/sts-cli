# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming, constructive, and useful to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Explain context, welcome questions, and assume good intent where reasonable.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the security of other people's systems.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, repeated disruptive behavior, or publishing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable. Neither is sharing credentials or customer data from systems you do not have permission to disclose.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to **support@muneris.dk**. Maintainers will review reports and may request changes, remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary. Reports will be handled with appropriate discretion; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where legal or safety obligations apply.

This policy applies to project-managed spaces and when representing the project publicly. Maintainers are expected to apply it fairly and proportionately.
